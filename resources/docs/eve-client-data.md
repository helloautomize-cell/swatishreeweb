## 7. Compliance flags (fix before any build)

1. **Patient Stories page (high risk).** Named patient quotes are testimonials, which Indian medical advertising rules prohibit. One quote also credits Dr. Swati's expertise for a medical outcome. The home page also plans a testimonial carousel. **Plan:** drop the page and carousel. Replace with an educational "Your fertility journey, explained" page with no patient quotes, and keep the "Ready to start" CTA.
2. **Banned wording in treatment copy.** "World-class IVF protocols" and "guiding you toward a successful, healthy pregnancy" will be rewritten. No outcome promises, no superlatives.
3. **Named hospitals.** Past roles and current visiting-consultant roles are factual credentials and can stay, once confirmed. The lines about "partnerships with Apollo Fertility Brookefield and Motherhood Fertility for advanced IVF lab procedures" imply referral arrangements. The site will say IVF lab procedures are available through associated centres, until she confirms in writing what can be stated.
4. **"ART Level 1 Certified Clinic".** Needs the certificate and registration number. Needs a legal check against current ART regulation. No sex-selection wording anywhere (PCPNDT).
5. **Reproductive immunology.** Evidence is contested. The doctor must approve the wording and the page stays neutral.
6. **Personal story.** Includes her child's health history. Written approval from her, kept in the client file.
7. **Contact form.** The supplied form collects health-related messages. It will follow the standard appointment form: unticked consent, guardian rule for under-18s, and "do not include detailed medical information".
8. **Writing style.** The drafts use many em dashes and some banned tone words ("world-class", "empowered", "navigate"). The content pass removes them.
9. **Naming.** The drafts use "EVE Women & Fertility Clinic", "EVE Women and Fertility Clinic", "EVE Fertility", "Dr. Swati" and "Dr. Swati Shree". Locked: "EVE Women and Fertility Clinic" and "Dr. Swati Shree".
