## 3. Work completed

- Rewrote **474 heading styles** across the 63 articles to natural sentence case — the single biggest "AI-written" signal on the site.
- Expanded **225 short FAQ answers** across 44 articles so each gives a complete, quotable 40–60-word answer (what Google and AI assistants prefer to quote).
- Repaired **2 comparison tables** that were showing as raw text instead of real tables (AMH vs egg quality, IUI vs IVF).
- Added **"Medically reviewed by Dr. Swati Shree" with full credentials to the top of all 63 articles** (previously only at the bottom).
- Fixed **internal linking**: 7 articles now link to their sibling guides, and the 6 endometriosis articles now link to IVF and fertility-evaluation service pages.
- Gave the **3 older articles real publication dates** — they previously showed an empty "Published ·" line.
- Sharpened the titles of **3 older articles** so they no longer compete with newer articles for the same search term.
- Added **visible source references** on 4 pages that only had them hidden in code.
- Lengthened **4 page titles** and **2 descriptions** that were too short for search display.
- Added **cost questions and answers** (honest "get a written estimate" wording, no made-up prices) to the IUI, egg-freezing, TESA/PESA and HSG pages.
- Technical: added the **web app manifest**, widened **robots.txt** to allow more AI crawlers (Claude-User, Perplexity-User, CCBot, anthropic-ai) while blocking internal pages, added a **breadcrumb to the blog index**, added **Wikipedia links to ~40 medical topics in the structured data**, and fixed a **Windows build bug** that could falsely report 114 broken links.

- **Stronger medical trust signals for Google** — every article now clearly shows who wrote it, who reviewed it, when, and the reviewer's credentials. This matters most for health topics.
- **Better chance of being quoted in AI answers** — complete FAQ answers, working tables, and Wikipedia-linked medical entities make your pages easier for ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews to cite.
